# Profit Analysis

## Purpose

Calculate whether the product can achieve the target profit margin.

---

## Selling Price

### Amazon Selling Price

Input:

- Selling Price (USD)

Example:

- $49.99
- $69.99
- $99.99

---

## Product Cost

### Factory Cost

Include:

- Product cost
- Packaging cost
- Spare parts cost

Example:

Factory Cost = $12.00

---

## Shipping Cost

### Ocean Freight

Include:

- Freight
- Customs clearance
- Truck delivery

Example:

Shipping Cost = $4.00

### Express Shipping

Example:

Shipping Cost = $12.00

---

## Amazon Fees

### Referral Fee

Typical:

- 15%

Formula:

Referral Fee = Selling Price × 15%

---

### FBA Fee

Input:

- Product size
- Product weight

Example:

FBA Fee = $8.00

---

## Advertising Cost

### PPC Cost

Target:

- 10% ~ 15%

Formula:

Advertising Cost = Selling Price × Advertising Rate

---

## Total Cost

Formula:

Total Cost =
Factory Cost
+ Shipping Cost
+ Amazon Referral Fee
+ FBA Fee
+ Advertising Cost

---

## Net Profit

Formula:

Net Profit =
Selling Price
- Total Cost

---

## Net Margin

Formula:

Net Margin =
Net Profit ÷ Selling Price × 100%

---

## ROI

Formula:

ROI =
Net Profit ÷ Product Cost × 100%

---

## Evaluation Standard

### Excellent

- Net Margin > 25%
- ROI > 100%

Recommendation:
Strongly Recommend

---

### Good

- Net Margin 15%~25%
- ROI 50%~100%

Recommendation:
Recommend

---

### Average

- Net Margin 10%~15%
- ROI 30%~50%

Recommendation:
Cautious

---

### Poor

- Net Margin < 10%
- ROI < 30%

Recommendation:
Not Recommend
