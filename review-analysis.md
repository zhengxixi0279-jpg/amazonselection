# Review Analysis

## Purpose

Analyze customer reviews and identify product improvement opportunities.

---

## Positive Reviews Analysis

### Collect

- Most frequently mentioned advantages
- Features customers like most
- Reasons for high ratings

### Output

- Core selling points
- Marketing keywords
- Features to keep

---

## Negative Reviews Analysis

### Collect

- Most frequently mentioned complaints
- Product defects
- Packaging issues
- Shipping issues

### Output

- Improvement opportunities
- Risk points
- Features to redesign

---

## Review Metrics

### Excellent

- Rating > 4.3
- Negative review ratio < 10%

### Average

- Rating 4.0 ~ 4.3
- Negative review ratio 10% ~ 20%

### Poor

- Rating < 4.0
- Negative review ratio > 20%

---

## Common Complaint Categories

### Product Quality

- Broken parts
- Rust
- Poor welding
- Material too thin

### Packaging

- Damaged during shipping
- Missing accessories

### Installation

- Difficult assembly
- Missing instructions

### Performance

- Does not work as expected
- Poor durability
